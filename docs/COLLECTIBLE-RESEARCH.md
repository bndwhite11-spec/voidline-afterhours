## Small-scale and expression guidance

- **Stirner 20px:** reserve negative space beside the nose; one spectacle circle
  and the tall collar matter more than texture. Keep hatching for inspection.
- **Curtains 20px:** the frame and curtain gap must remain separate. One dark
  spectacle mass can survive; a fully drawn room cannot.
- **Alien 20px:** prioritize the elongated skull, paired eyes and tapered jaw.
  A generic round green face loses the particular reference.
- **XRP 20px:** the open center and separation of its opposed curves must survive.
  A filled X or three droplets is incorrect.
- **LARP 20px:** retain a shield/visor silhouette and one obvious homemade repair.
  Corrugation, stitches and tape fibers belong in larger variants.
- **Hands 20px:** avoid tiny finger count noise. Palm direction, one thumb and
  the opposing up/down positions identify the gesture.

Material detail needs specific construction, not a universal bevel. Portrait
ink, cloth folds, a wax impression, enamel face, glass thickness and metal edge
planes should use different shapes and contrast. Static poses must read before
animation; named joint layers must explain the action rather than applying one
floating/scaling preset to unrelated objects.

### Visible lessons and original VOIDLINE translation

The sampled official demonstration gives the object a clear, isolated focal
stage. Its illustration has legible volume, controlled bright edges and a
recognizable character/prop before the surrounding attributes are read. The
received-item sheet places a large object above a concise title and a plain
metadata table; its primary action is visually separate from the artwork.
The chat card and the inspection sheet use related artwork at different scales.

Apply that care to VOIDLINE's authored objects: let the rose/cassette/case fill a
generous stage, preserve a strong resting silhouette, and use real petals, reels,
clasps, covers or facial parts for the action. Do not replace object craft with
sparkles, a universal floating animation or the same metal frame. Keep subdued
shell surfaces while allowing each object its own material and restrained
palette. Names and acquisition facts belong in real text outside the drawing.

Use the collection/profile relationship as inspiration: an optional owned gift
shelf can lead to inspection, and theme/type/owned filters should make browsing
predictable. Keep Browse, an owned instance, a pending gift request and an
accepted receipt semantically distinct. Preserve profile visibility and sender
choices rather than inferring consent from a decorative display.

Telegram's paid upgrades, randomized collectible traits, edition/rarity numbers,
market values, marketplaces and blockchain transfers are **not** being adopted.
VOIDLINE's initial catalog remains free, with original fixed artwork and its
existing authoritative grant/accept/transfer model. Pure animation preview must
remain available without claiming or transferring anything; a receipt reveal
still requires an acknowledged owned instance.
