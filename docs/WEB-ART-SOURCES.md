# Site 32 web-source artwork

This record describes the 83 Site 32 compositions. The current mixed-format catalog preserves these source credits and adds original layered art. [Gift artwork and motion](COLLECTIBLE-ART.md)

Site 32 contains 49 decorations, 32 gifts and 2 earned-award images. Its credited sources include downloaded photographs, human illustrations, official marks and published memes. Studio 01 retains the credited originals in eight object-specific compositions and adds separately drawn optical miniatures. [Studio 01 source and review notes](STUDIO-01.md) Sources are local at runtime. Preparation uses conventional crops, masks, rasterization, format conversion, palette optimization and disclosed collages. No image generation or generative fill is used.

The records in `src/art/web-*.mjs`, public `/art/v4/manifest.json`, `/art/sources/ATTRIBUTION.txt` and SVG descriptions carry attribution and changes. Unknown meme/brand reuse licenses are recorded as unknown, rather than an invented grant. Earlier synthetic vector bodies are retained as historical source; the Site 32 build required all 83 web entries. Catalog IDs and ownership remain unchanged.

Midnight Tape now has a specific tape-check reveal: two circular regions from
its actual cassette artwork turn independently while the case stays intact.
The original source pixels and credits remain unchanged. This1540ms sequence
runs once after deliberate preview or an acknowledged gift receipt; it returns
to the complete static pose. Existing reduced-motion, offscreen, close and
background cancellation apply. No continuous animation or new image download
is added.
